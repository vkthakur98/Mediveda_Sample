PROBLEMS WE TREAT

MALE FERTILITY

Varicocele

Varicocele is a medical condition in which the veins in man's scrotum become engorged with blood, causing them to swell up.

Oligospermia

Oligospermia states to a condition in which the semen has a low concentration of sperms. It is a common issue in male infertility.

Azoospermia

Azoospermia refers to the absence of sperm in a man's ejaculate, a condition that can make conception challenging.

Erectile Dysfunction

Erectile dysfunction is the inability to achieve or maintain an erection firm enough for satisfactory sexual performance.

Low Testosterone

Low testosterone levels can affect sperm production, sexual function and overall male reproductive health significantly.

LIVER CARE

Fatty Liver

Fatty liver occurs when excess fat builds up in the liver cells, affecting its normal function and overall health.

Elevated SGOT/SGPT

High SGOT and SGPT levels indicate liver inflammation or damage caused by toxins, infections, or lifestyle habits.

Alcoholic Liver Damage

Excessive alcohol intake can damage liver cells, leading to inflammation, scarring, and liver dysfunction.

Liver Cirrhosis

Cirrhosis is the advanced stage of liver scarring that can lead to liver failure if not treated on time.

Jaundice

Jaundice is a yellowing of the skin and eyes caused by high bilirubin levels, often signaling liver dysfunction.
